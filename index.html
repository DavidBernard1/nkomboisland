Welcome to Nkombo Island

Nkombo Island is one of Rwanda's largest inhabited islands located on beautiful Lake Kivu. Visitors enjoy Marchal Nkombo Beach, peaceful boat cruises, traditional fishing experiences, rich Amashi culture, local markets, delicious fresh fish, and unforgettable sunsets.

Top Attractions

Marchal Nkombo Beach

Crystal-clear water and relaxing sandy shoreline.

Cultural Experience

Traditional dance, music and island hospitality.

Fishing Tours

Join local fishermen on Lake Kivu.

Local Trading
